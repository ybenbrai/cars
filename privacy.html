Privacy Policy

Sunset Drift · effective 12 November 2026

Sunset Drift is a free racing game. You can play without an account: as a guest, your progress is saved only on your device and nothing is sent to us. This policy explains what happens when you create an account or race online.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no ad networks and no tracking or analytics SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with third parties.
  • No in-app purchases. Credits are earned in the game and have no real-world value.

Where the data lives

The game and its accounts run on Cloudflare (Workers and Durable Objects), which processes the data on our behalf. Data on your device (guest progress, settings, ghost laps, language) stays in your browser's storage until you clear it.

How long we keep it

Account data and cloud saves are kept until you delete your account. Sign-in sessions expire after 30 days. An administrator may suspend or remove accounts that break the rules, for example offensive usernames or cheating in online races.

Delete your account

In the game, open your account (top bar) → Delete my account, and confirm with your password. Your account, cloud save and username are deleted immediately and permanently. You can still keep playing as a guest.

Children

Sunset Drift does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Children can play as guests without giving any personal data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights and contact

You can access your data in the game, and delete it at any time as described above. For any other privacy request or question, open an issue at github.com/ybenbrai/cars/issues.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
